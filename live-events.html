Live Events

The art of the occasion.

From swift, spirited sketches that reach every guest to full-length fashion portraits worthy of a gallery wall, choose the style that suits your celebration, or blend several across the evening.

i.Quick Sketches ii.Portrait Sketches iii.Full-Length Fashion iv.Brand Activations
i.

Quick Portrait Sketches

Lively, confident head-and-shoulders sketches in ink and ink wash, with a touch of alcohol marker. Ideal for large guest lists and cocktail hours when you want everyone to leave with something.

  • Time per guest3–5 minutes
  • Guests per hour12–15 per artist
  • MediumInk & ink wash
  • Best forWeddings, parties, large events
ii.

Portrait Sketches

A more considered bust portrait with richer color, texture, and likeness. A refined keepsake for intimate weddings, rehearsal dinners, and VIP lounges.

  • Time per guest8–12 minutes
  • Guests per hour5–7 per artist
  • MediumInk, ink wash & alcohol marker
  • Best forIntimate weddings, showers, VIP areas
iii.

Full-Length Fashion Portraits

Head to toe, outfit and all. Our signature fashion illustration captures the gown, the tailoring, and the pose. It’s the portrait for the bride, the bridal party, and best-dressed guests.

  • Time per guest15–20 minutes
  • Guests per hour3–4 per artist
  • MediumInk, ink wash & alcohol marker
  • Best forBridal parties, fashion & luxury retail
iv.

Brand Activations & Bespoke

Portraits printed onto custom branded cards, live product sketching, illustrated menus and signage, or a large-format piece drawn over the course of the event. Built to be photographed, posted, and tagged.

  • FormatCustom branded cards & frames
  • Add-onsProduct sketches, live murals
  • DeliverablesDigital scans for your socials
  • Best forLaunches, pop-ups, conferences
